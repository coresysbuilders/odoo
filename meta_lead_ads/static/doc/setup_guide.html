Connecting Odoo to Meta

A step-by-step guide to the Connect to Meta wizard (Settings → Meta Lead Ads → Connect to Meta) and where each credential comes from in Meta.

The wizard's first screen asks for three things:

Wizard fieldWhat it isWhere it comes from
AppYour Meta App ID (a number)Meta for Developers → App settings → Basic
App SecretYour Meta App secretMeta for Developers → App settings → Basic
TokenAn access tokenBusiness Settings → System Users (recommended)

The App ID and secret live in the Meta for Developers dashboard; the token comes from your Business Settings. They are two different places.

1. App ID and App Secret

  1. Go to https://developers.facebook.com → My Apps. If you don't have an app yet, click Create App and choose the Business type.
  2. Open your app and go to App settings → Basic in the left menu.
  3. Copy the App ID shown at the top and paste it into the wizard's App field.
  4. Next to it, click Show on the App secret (Meta asks for your password) and paste it into the wizard's App Secret field.

While you are here, add the Webhooks product to the app (left menu → Add Product → Webhooks). You will need it for the leadgen subscription at the end.

2. Access token

A System User token is recommended because it is long-lived and not tied to a personal login.

  1. Go to https://business.facebook.com → Business settings (the gear).
  2. Open Users → System Users, then create one (or select an existing one) and give it the Admin or Employee role.
  3. Click Add Assets and assign your Page(s), with full control / manage leads, and your App from step 1.
  4. Click Generate new token, choose your App, and select these permissions: leads_retrieval, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_metadata.
  5. Choose an expiration (60 days or Never), generate the token, and copy it immediately: Meta only shows it once.
  6. Paste it into the wizard's Token field and leave "This is a short-lived User token (exchange it)" unchecked.

Quick alternative: a short-lived token

For a quick test you can take a temporary token from the Graph API Explorer (https://developers.facebook.com/tools/explorer): select your app, add the four permissions above and click Generate Access Token. This token expires in about 1–2 hours, so in the wizard tick "This is a short-lived User token (exchange it)" and the module exchanges it for a long-lived one automatically.

3. Keep everything under the same Business

The app and the Page must belong to the same Business. In Business settings → Accounts you should see both your Pages and your App; add or claim them if they are missing. The token's System User must have both assigned to it (step 2.3).

4. Development mode vs. real leads

While the app is in Development mode, leads_retrieval only returns test leads. Real production leads require:

  • App Review → Advanced Access for leads_retrieval (plus pages_show_list, pages_manage_metadata and pages_read_engagement), and
  • Business Verification.
This is a separate Meta approval process and it takes time, so start it early. The wizard still validates the token and imports your Pages and Forms before approval; it simply shows a "Development mode, test leads only" status until Advanced Access is granted.

5. Finish in the wizard

  1. Click Validate & Discover. The wizard checks the token and lists your Pages.
  2. Select the Pages to sync and continue; the wizard finds their Lead Forms for review.
  3. Click Finish. The wizard then shows a Webhook URL and a Verify Token (visible to Meta Admins only). Keep this screen open: you paste both values into your Meta app in the next step.

6. Set up the webhook in your Meta app

A leadgen webhook has two parts, and you need both:

  • App level: tell Meta where to send events (your Odoo callback URL) and which field (leadgen). You do this once, by hand, in the App dashboard (below).
  • Page level: subscribe the app to each Page's leads. The Odoo wizard does this for you when you click Subscribe.

The webhook setting lives in the Meta for Developers App dashboard (developers.facebook.com), not in Business Suite or Business Settings, and on most apps Webhooks is a product you must add before it appears in the menu.

  1. Go to https://developers.facebook.com → My Apps and open your app.
  2. In the left sidebar, look for Webhooks under Products. If it isn't there, click Add Product at the bottom of the sidebar (or the "Add products to your app" card), find Webhooks and click Set up.
  3. Open Webhooks. At the top, change the object dropdown (it often defaults to User) to Page, then click Subscribe to this object.
  4. In the dialog, paste the two values from the wizard's final screen: Callback URL = the Webhook URL (https://<your-odoo-domain>/meta_lead_ads/webhook), and Verify Token = the Verify Token.
  5. Click Verify and Save. Meta calls that URL straight away to confirm it, so your Odoo must be reachable over public HTTPS.
  6. In the Page object's field list, find leadgen and click Subscribe.

Then, back in the Odoo wizard, click Subscribe to complete the page level.

Can't find Webhooks at all? Make sure you are on developers.facebook.com (the App dashboard), not business.facebook.com (Business Suite), and that the app is a Business-type app.

Once both parts are in place, new lead submissions arrive in CRM → Leads within a minute or two, and the backfill job picks up anything the webhook misses.

Finding the webhook URL and verify token later

The wizard's final screen disappears once you close it, but the values are not lost: the verify token is stored permanently and the callback URL is fixed.

  1. Turn on developer mode: Settings → General Settings, scroll to the bottom, Activate the developer mode.
  2. Go to Settings → Technical → Parameters → System Parameters.
  3. Verify Token: search for meta_lead_ads.webhook_verify_token; its Value is your verify token. It is generated once and never changes.
  4. Callback URL: always <web.base.url>/meta_lead_ads/webhook. Look up the key web.base.url in the same list and append /meta_lead_ads/webhook.

Alternatively, re-open Settings → Meta Lead Ads → Connect to Meta and run it through to Finish again. It reuses the same verify token and shows both values again, so a webhook you already configured in Meta stays valid.

Troubleshooting

  • "That token is invalid or expired." The token was mistyped, has expired, or lacks the required permissions. Generate a fresh System User token with the four permissions listed above.
  • "Meta did not return a token owner id." Use a System User token (Business Manager), not a plain app or page token.
  • Status stays "Development mode, test leads only." The token is valid but leads_retrieval doesn't have Advanced Access yet; complete App Review and Business Verification.
  • No leads arriving. First check the Scheduler status on the Meta account form (Settings → Meta Lead Ads → Open Accounts, open an account, or click Check Scheduler). Leads only sync if Odoo's scheduler runs the jobs; if it shows Not running, the server needs db_name set (a dbfilter alone is not enough), max_cron_threads above 0 and no --no-cron, then a restart. If the scheduler is healthy, confirm the Page is subscribed to leadgen (wizard Subscribe button) and that the Webhook URL is reachable from the internet over HTTPS.
