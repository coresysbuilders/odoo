Connecting Odoo to Meta

A step-by-step guide to the Connect to Meta wizard (Settings → Meta Lead Ads → Connect to Meta) and where each credential comes from in Meta.

The wizard's first screen asks for three things:

Wizard fieldWhat it isWhere it comes from
AppYour Meta App ID (a number)Meta for Developers → App settings → Basic
App SecretYour Meta App secretMeta for Developers → App settings → Basic
TokenAn access tokenBusiness Settings → System Users (recommended)

The App ID and secret live in the Meta for Developers dashboard; the token comes from your Business Settings. They are two different places.

On a database with several companies the first screen also has a Company field; see Which company receives the leads. A Meta Admin who can't open Settings finds the same section, with the Connect to Meta button, under Meta Leads → Settings.

After the connection steps, this guide covers companies, Page routing, System Health, recovering failed leads, archived Pages and personal data and retention, then troubleshooting.

1. App ID and App Secret

  1. Go to https://developers.facebook.com → My Apps. If you don't have an app yet, click Create App and choose the Business type.
  2. Open your app and go to App settings → Basic in the left menu.
  3. Copy the App ID shown at the top and paste it into the wizard's App field.
  4. Next to it, click Show on the App secret (Meta asks for your password) and paste it into the wizard's App Secret field.

While you are here, add the Webhooks product to the app (left menu → Add Product → Webhooks). You will need it for the leadgen subscription at the end.

2. Access token

A System User token is recommended because it is long-lived and not tied to a personal login.

  1. Go to https://business.facebook.com → Business settings (the gear).
  2. Open Users → System Users, then create one (or select an existing one) and give it the Admin or Employee role.
  3. Click Add Assets and assign your Page(s), with full control / manage leads, and your App from step 1.
  4. Click Generate new token, choose your App, and select these permissions: leads_retrieval, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_metadata.
  5. Choose an expiration (60 days or Never), generate the token, and copy it immediately: Meta only shows it once.
  6. Paste it into the wizard's Token field and leave "This is a short-lived User token (exchange it)" unchecked.

Quick alternative: a short-lived token

For a quick test you can take a temporary token from the Graph API Explorer (https://developers.facebook.com/tools/explorer): select your app, add the four permissions above and click Generate Access Token. This token expires in about 1–2 hours, so in the wizard tick "This is a short-lived User token (exchange it)" and the module exchanges it for a long-lived one automatically.

3. Keep everything under the same Business

The app and the Page must belong to the same Business. In Business settings → Accounts you should see both your Pages and your App; add or claim them if they are missing. The token's System User must have both assigned to it (step 2.3).

4. Development mode vs. real leads

While the app is in Development mode, leads_retrieval only returns test leads. Real production leads require:

  • App Review → Advanced Access for leads_retrieval (plus pages_show_list, pages_manage_metadata and pages_read_engagement), and
  • Business Verification.
This is a separate Meta approval process and it takes time, so start it early. The wizard still validates the token and imports your Pages and Forms before approval; it simply shows a "Development mode, test leads only" status until Advanced Access is granted.

5. Finish in the wizard

  1. Click Validate & Discover. The wizard checks the token and lists your Pages.
  2. Select the Pages to sync and continue; the wizard finds their Lead Forms for review.
  3. Click Finish. The wizard then shows a Webhook URL and a Verify Token (visible to Meta Admins only). Keep this screen open: you paste both values into your Meta app in the next step.

6. Set up the webhook in your Meta app

A leadgen webhook has two parts, and you need both:

  • App level: tell Meta where to send events (your Odoo callback URL) and which field (leadgen). You do this once, by hand, in the App dashboard (below).
  • Page level: subscribe the app to each Page's leads. The Odoo wizard does this for you when you click Subscribe.

The webhook setting lives in the Meta for Developers App dashboard (developers.facebook.com), not in Business Suite or Business Settings, and on most apps Webhooks is a product you must add before it appears in the menu.

  1. Go to https://developers.facebook.com → My Apps and open your app.
  2. In the left sidebar, look for Webhooks under Products. If it isn't there, click Add Product at the bottom of the sidebar (or the "Add products to your app" card), find Webhooks and click Set up.
  3. Open Webhooks. At the top, change the object dropdown (it often defaults to User) to Page, then click Subscribe to this object.
  4. In the dialog, paste the two values from the wizard's final screen: Callback URL = the Webhook URL (https://<your-odoo-domain>/meta_lead_ads/webhook), and Verify Token = the Verify Token.
  5. Click Verify and Save. Meta calls that URL straight away to confirm it, so your Odoo must be reachable over public HTTPS.
  6. In the Page object's field list, find leadgen and click Subscribe.

Then, back in the Odoo wizard, click Subscribe to complete the page level.

The wizard's Webhook Subscription status reads Subscribed only when your app is subscribed to the Page's leadgen field. Another app subscribed means a different Meta app gets the Page's leads but yours doesn't, Not subscribed means your app doesn't get them, and Couldn't check means Odoo couldn't read the status from Meta. A hint under the status says what to do; click Refresh status to ask Meta again.

Can't find Webhooks at all? Make sure you are on developers.facebook.com (the App dashboard), not business.facebook.com (Business Suite), and that the app is a Business-type app.

Once both parts are in place, new lead submissions usually reach CRM → Leads within a few minutes, and the backfill job picks up leads the webhook missed, as long as Meta still holds them.

Finding the webhook URL and verify token later

The wizard's final screen disappears once you close it, but you can look both values up again: the verify token is stored in Odoo and the callback URL is fixed.

  1. Turn on developer mode: Settings → General Settings, scroll to the bottom, Activate the developer mode.
  2. Go to Settings → Technical → Parameters → System Parameters.
  3. Verify Token: search for meta_lead_ads.webhook_verify_token; its Value is your verify token. It is generated once and stays the same while the module is installed.
  4. Callback URL: always <web.base.url>/meta_lead_ads/webhook. Look up the key web.base.url in the same list and append /meta_lead_ads/webhook.

Alternatively, re-open Settings → Meta Lead Ads → Connect to Meta and run it through to Finish again. It reuses the same verify token and shows both values again, so a webhook you already configured in Meta stays valid.

Which company receives the leads

This only matters on a database with several companies; the company fields below are shown to users who work in more than one company.

  1. When you connect. On the first screen of Connect to Meta, set Company to the company that should receive the leads of this connection. It must be a company you belong to, and it must be selected in the company switcher.
  2. For an account already connected. Running the wizard again doesn't change the account's company, even if you pick another one on its first screen; its last screen says so. To change it, open Settings → Meta Lead Ads → Open Accounts, open the account and change Company.
  3. For one Page. On the account, click the Pages button and open the Page. Set Company Override to send this Page's leads to another company, or leave it empty to use the account's company. Lead Company shows where the Page's leads go.

The lead company decides more than where a lead is created. Duplicate checks look only at leads of that company or with no company. New-lead notifications go only to users who can open the lead. The Meta Leads dashboard counts the companies selected in the company switcher. A change of company applies to leads that arrive after it; leads already in CRM keep their company.

Routing a Page's leads

Open the Page (Settings → Meta Lead Ads → Open Accounts, open the account, then the Pages button). The Lead Routing section is shown to Meta Admins:

FieldWhat it does
Create AsNew leads from this Page are created as a Lead or as an Opportunity. A new Page starts on Lead, or on Opportunity when the Leads feature is turned off in CRM. A submission matched to an existing lead keeps that lead's type.
Default SalespersonAssigned to new leads from this Page. It must be an active internal user with Sales rights who can work in the Page's lead company. Leave it empty and new leads arrive unassigned.
Default Sales TeamSet on new leads from this Page. It must be active, and belong to the lead company or to no company. With no salesperson set, the team's own assignment rules can pick the lead up. Left empty, a lead with a default salesperson gets that salesperson's team.

When a submission matches an existing open lead, that lead only gets the salesperson and team if it has none. If a default stops fitting later, for example because the user was archived, lost Sales rights or left the company, or the team doesn't use leads or the pipeline, the Page form shows a warning that says what is wrong and what new leads get until you change it.

Form answers can't fill the lead's workflow fields: the title, salesperson, sales team, stage, probability, priority, expected and recurring revenue, expected closing, lost reason, customer, the UTM campaign, source and medium, or any other link to a user or contact. A field mapping onto one of them is refused. Mappings like that from an older version were switched off on upgrade; the Field Mappings list shows them under the Disabled filter, with the reason.

Reading System Health

Open Meta Leads → Dashboard. The System Health card has four indicators, each with a status and a short caption. They don't change with the selected period. Webhook, Queue drain and Backfill cover the whole database, because there is one webhook and one set of scheduled jobs; Token follows the companies selected in the company switcher.

IndicatorStatusWhat it means
WebhookRunningA webhook lead was processed in the last 24 hours.
Quiet or No webhook leads yetNo webhook lead was processed in the last 24 hours, or none is recorded yet. See No leads arriving.
Receiving, but failingWebhook leads failed after the last success (or in the last 7 days, when no success is recorded). Open the Sync Log for the error, then see Recovering failed leads.
Falling behindWebhook events have waited more than 10 minutes for the queue drain. Leads Meta asked Odoo to hold back are not counted.
Caption: leads waiting on an archived PageLeads held because their Page is archived. They don't turn the indicator red; see Archived Pages.
Queue drain and BackfillRunning, Waiting for first run or Falling behindThe job runs. A fresh install waits a few minutes for the first run, and Falling behind (queue drain only) means webhook events are waiting.
Not running or DisabledThe job hasn't run when it should have, or its scheduled action is turned off. Click Check Scheduler on the Meta account; it names the job that stopped.
TokenValid, Expiring soon or InvalidThe worst token among the active accounts. Expiring soon means one expires within 7 days; generate a new token and run Connect to Meta with it. Invalid also shows when there is no active account. The caption says when the tokens were last checked.

The Meta account form shows the same scheduler status as a badge next to its connection status, with Test Connection and Check Scheduler buttons.

Recovering failed leads

A lead that fails to come in leaves a Failed row in the Sync Log (Settings → Meta Lead Ads → Open Sync Logs) with its Page, Meta Lead ID and Error Message. Webhook deliveries also have a row in the webhook queue, CRM → Configuration → Webhook Events.

  1. Fix the cause first if the error names one: renew the token with Connect to Meta, add a missing permission in Meta, or reactivate the Page.
  2. Retry from the Sync Log. Open the row and click Retry, or select several rows and choose Actions > Retry Selected. Each click handles a limited batch; the notification says how many succeeded, how many still failed and how many are left for another click.
  3. Or retry from the webhook queue. Retry works the same on a failed event. On an event that is waiting for Meta (its Next Attempt is set), Retry ends the wait now, and with it any lead read pause on its Page.
  4. A row without a Meta Lead ID is about a whole lead form, for example a listing Meta returned incomplete. Retry on it asks the backfill to read that form again.

Rate limits. When Meta asks Odoo to slow down, the event waits until the time Meta gives, up to a day. Meta can also pause lead reads for one Page (shown as Lead Reads Paused Until on the Page form) or for the whole app (Lead reads paused in the Meta Lead Ads settings). Leads resume on their own when the pause ends. Resume Lead Reads on the Page, or Resume lead reads in the settings, ends the pause sooner, but reading again too early can make Meta extend the block.

Retry is refused for a lead whose personal data was erased, and it waits while the lead's Page is archived (see below).

Leads deleted or merged in Odoo. The webhook, Retry and the scheduled syncs skip them, so they don't come back on their own. CRM → Configuration → Removed Meta Leads lists them for 200 days. Restore fetches a deleted lead from Meta again; a merged lead can't be restored while the lead it was merged into exists, and an erased one can't be restored.

A single lead by ID. CRM → Configuration → Ingest Meta Lead by ID fetches one Meta lead through the same ingestion.

Archived Pages

Archiving a Page in Odoo stops the backfill from reading its forms. Webhook leads for it are not fetched: they wait in the Sync Log as failed until you reactivate the Page, with the message "This Page is archived in Odoo. Reactivate it, then Retry." Retry is refused while the Page stays archived. The dashboard's Webhook caption counts these leads without turning the indicator red.

To bring the Page back, unarchive it, or run Connect to Meta and tick it on the Pages step (archived Pages start unticked). It returns with its routing and settings. On the next step you choose which of its archived lead forms come back, with their field mappings and sync position. The held leads then go back in the queue and are fetched again on their own; their rows say "Queued again after its Page was reactivated."

Personal data and retention

Who sees what. The Meta Answers tab of a lead follows the lead's access rules: someone who can't open the lead can't read its answers either. Answers and names are shown as text, not HTML, in the lead description and in notifications. Raw Meta payloads, the App Secret and tokens are shown to Meta Admins only.

Erasing a person's data. A Meta Admin can:

  • click Erase Personal Data on a Meta lead, or select up to 200 leads in CRM → Leads or the Pipeline list view and click Erase Personal Data. Contact details, mapped answers, the Meta answer block, crm's merge notes and the stored Meta payloads are removed, for the selected leads and for Meta leads merged into them. The stage, salesperson, revenue and campaign attribution stay, and the linked contact and hand-written messages are not changed, so handle those separately. The lead shows Personal Data Erased On, and it can't be retried;
  • erase sync rows and webhook rows that didn't become a lead with the Erase Personal Data button in the Sync Logs or Webhook Events list, or on the row itself. The automatic syncs won't bring that lead back.

Deleting a Meta lead in Odoo also removes its stored Meta payloads.

Retention. Under Settings → Meta Lead Ads → Data retention:

SettingWhat it removesDefault
Sync log retentionSuccessful and skipped sync rows. The dashboard keeps their counts.14 days
Processed webhook rowsWebhook events that were processed. Waiting events are kept.14 days
Failed rowsFailed webhook events, and the stored Meta data of failed sync rows. The failed sync row itself stays and can still be retried while Meta holds the lead.90 days

Each period is from 1 to 3650 days, and the cleanup runs daily. Retention covers the module's logs and queue only: leads stay in CRM until you erase or delete them.

One Meta app per database

Odoo checks the signature of every webhook call from Meta with one App Secret, so a database can be connected to one Meta app only. Older versions of this module let you connect accounts of more than one app. If your database still has active accounts of two or more apps, the Meta account form and the Meta Lead Ads settings show a warning with the number of apps, and Odoo refuses to reconnect or unarchive an account until only one app is left.

  1. See which accounts belong to which app. Select every company that has Meta accounts in the company switcher, then open Meta Leads > Settings > Open Accounts. In the search bar, open Group By > Add Custom Group and choose App ID.
  2. Pick the app to keep. This is usually the app whose webhook is set up in Meta and that most of your Pages use.
  3. Archive every account of the other apps. Open each one and click Actions > Archive (the gear icon). Their Pages, lead forms and field mappings are kept.
  4. Once the active accounts all use one app, the warning disappears.

To bring back an archived account of an old app, connect it again under the current app: run Connect to Meta with a token of the same owner (the same System User), generated for the current app. The wizard reactivates the account with the current App ID and keeps its Pages and lead forms. A plain Unarchive is refused, because the account still holds the old App ID.

Uninstalling

Uninstalling removes promoted fields and their values; export them first. The fields affected are the ones the module created when you promoted an answer, plus any custom field whose technical name starts with x_meta_ and that a Meta field mapping fills.

The Meta details on each lead (campaign, ad set, ad and form IDs and names, submission time, stored Meta answers) and the module's sync logs and settings are removed too, and the Instagram source and Paid Social medium it added are deleted, which leaves leads that used them with an empty Source and Medium. Leads themselves stay in CRM. Other custom fields are kept, except a custom field that is computed from, related to, or the one2many inverse of a promoted field: Odoo removes that field and its values together with the promoted field, so export it too. A promoted field that another module, such as Studio, also owns is kept. If you customized a view to show a promoted field, check that view afterwards: Odoo removes the field but can leave the view pointing at it, so edit the view to take the field out.

Troubleshooting

  • "That token is invalid or expired." The token was mistyped, has expired, or lacks the required permissions. Generate a fresh System User token with the four permissions listed above.
  • "Meta did not return a token owner id." Use a System User token (Business Manager), not a plain app or page token.
  • Status stays "Development mode, test leads only." The token is valid but leads_retrieval doesn't have Advanced Access yet; complete App Review and Business Verification.
  • No leads arriving. First open the Meta Leads dashboard and look at System Health. Webhook shows whether webhook leads are being processed. Quiet means Odoo hasn't processed a webhook lead in the last 24 hours. That is normal on a low-traffic form, but rejected calls from Meta (for example after an App Secret change) and a turned-off subscription look the same, so check the Webhooks page of your Meta app if you expect leads. Receiving, but failing means they arrive and fail (open the Sync Log for the error). Queue drain and Backfill show each scheduled job on its own. If either says Not running, check the Scheduler status on the Meta account form (or click Check Scheduler); it names the job that stopped. If both stopped, the server's Odoo needs db_name set (a dbfilter alone is not enough), max_cron_threads above 0, and no --no-cron, then a restart. If they're running, confirm the Page is subscribed to the leadgen field (wizard Subscribe button) and that the Webhook URL is reachable from the internet over HTTPS.
  • Leads of an archived Page. Leads for a Page you archived in Odoo wait in the Sync Log as failed until you reactivate the Page; they are then fetched again on their own.
  • Webhook or Queue drain shows Falling behind. Webhook events have waited more than 10 minutes. Check that Queue drain is running. If Meta paused lead reads, the Page shows Lead Reads Paused Until and the settings show Lead reads paused; leads resume when the pause ends (see Rate limits).
  • Retry says some rows were held back because Meta asked to pause lead reads. Wait for the pause to end, or resume lead reads on the Page or in the settings.
  • "The personal data of this Meta lead was erased, so it can't be retried." An erased lead isn't fetched again. If the person submits the form again, Meta sends a new lead.
  • A lead you deleted doesn't come back. Deleted and merged leads are skipped on purpose. Restore a deleted one from CRM → Configuration → Removed Meta Leads.
  • Leads land in the wrong company. Check Company on the Meta account and Company Override on the Page; Lead Company on the Page shows where its leads go. Leads already created keep their company.
  • New leads arrive without a salesperson or team. Set Default Salesperson and Default Sales Team on the Page. If they are set, read the warning on the Page form: the user may be archived, lack Sales rights or not work in the lead company.
  • A field mapping was switched off. Its target is a workflow field that form answers can no longer fill. Open the Field Mappings list with the Disabled filter to see the reason, and map the question to another field.
